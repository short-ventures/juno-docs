Most marketing work lives across too many tools: CRM, ad accounts, inboxes, spreadsheets, analytics and content systems.

Connectors let Juno work with those tools using the access your workspace has approved.

This means Juno can use real campaign data, customer context, and workspace records instead of relying only on pasted text.

## What it is for

- Searching and reading private workspace data
- Pulling reports from ad, analytics, CRM, and content systems
- Updating connected tools when you approve the work
- Sending handoffs through communication tools
- Combining several systems in one task

## What to expect

Juno will ask for a connector or credential when a task needs a tool that is not connected yet.

Connector availability depends on your workspace settings, plan, and connected credentials. Give Juno the tool name, record, account, date range, and output you want when you can.
