---

Connectors let Juno use the systems your team already works in. They give Juno access to the right tool for the task, such as a workspace app, ad account, CRM, spreadsheet, email inbox, analytics tool, or content system.

## What connectors are for

- Searching and reading private workspace data
- Updating connected tools when you approve the work
- Pulling reports from ad, analytics, CRM, and content systems
- Sending messages or handoffs through communication tools
- Combining context from several tools in one delegated task

## Examples

Juno can work with connectors such as Notion, Meta Ads, Google Sheets, Slack, HubSpot, Gmail, Google Analytics, Webflow, Shopify, and more.

Connector availability depends on your workspace settings, plan, and connected credentials.

## Good connector tasks

- Review last week's Meta Ads performance and summarize the biggest issues.
- Find the latest launch notes in Notion and turn them into a checklist.
- Pull rows from a Google Sheet and group the accounts that need follow-up.
- Draft a Slack update from the task summary.

If Juno needs access to a tool that has not been connected yet, it will ask for the missing connector or credential.
