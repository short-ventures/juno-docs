Most AI work only starts when someone remembers to ask for it.

Juno can schedule work for later or repeat it on a cadence.

This means recurring reports, reminders, audits, monitors, and routine summaries can happen without you rebuilding the request each time.

## What it is for

- Weekly or monthly reporting
- Recurring audits and monitors
- Follow-up reminders
- Routine data pulls and summaries
- Scheduled checks across websites, campaigns, or connected tools

## What to expect

Describe the task, schedule, source, and expected output. Narrow scopes work best, especially when a task repeats.

Scheduled work should have a clear review path. If a scheduled task needs a connector or changing source, Juno may ask for updated access or instructions.
