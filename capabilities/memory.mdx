Most AI tools forget the operating context your team repeats every week.

Juno uses workspace memory to keep durable context available for future tasks.

This means brand guidance, company facts, reusable sources, and lessons from previous work can carry forward without being pasted into every chat.

## What it is for

- Brand voice and style guidance
- Important company facts
- Reusable files and source materials
- Tool and connector context
- Lessons from previous work

## What to expect

Juno should remember durable guidance, not every temporary instruction. Tell it when a fact, preference, or source has changed.

Good memory is specific and useful across more than one task. Avoid storing one-off directions that only apply to the current request.
