Juno can use email for communication and workflow handoffs when the relevant mailbox or connector is connected.

## Common email workflows

- Search and summarize email threads
- Draft replies or follow-ups
- Send task artifacts by email
- Track replies to delegated work
- Combine email context with CRM, spreadsheet, or workspace data

## Good instructions

Name the audience, tone, source material, and approval requirements. If Juno should not send without review, say so in the task.
