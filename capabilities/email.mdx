Most email tools help you write messages, but the useful work is often in the thread context, follow-up, and handoff.

Juno can use email as a source, destination, and follow-up channel when the right mailbox or connector is connected.

This means you can ask it to summarize threads, draft replies, route updates, or prepare customer and teammate communication as part of a larger task.

## What it is for

- Searching and summarizing email threads
- Drafting replies and follow-ups
- Sending approved task artifacts by email
- Tracking replies to delegated work
- Combining email context with CRM, spreadsheet, and workspace data

## What to expect

Juno will not always need email, but it can use it when the conversation history or send path matters.

Tell Juno who the message is for, what tone to use, what source material to rely on, and whether it should wait for approval before sending.
