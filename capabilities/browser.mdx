Most AI tools can answer questions about the web, but they cannot reliably inspect the page in front of them.

Juno can use a browser when a task needs to look at websites, forms, pages, or browser-based tools directly.

This means you can ask it to research competitors, check a page, gather source material, or work through a web workflow without giving step-by-step clicks.

## Use cases

- Automating website workflows 
- Scraping data from directories
- Authenticating on private websites
- Filling in forms and registrations
- Analyzing the visual appearance of websites
