Juno can use a browser to inspect public pages, navigate web apps, capture page context, and complete browser-based workflows.

## Common browser tasks

- Research a website or competitor
- Inspect landing pages and forms
- Gather information from public web pages
- Check whether a page, flow, or asset renders correctly
- Use a connected web app when the workspace has the right access

## Tips

Give Juno the exact URL, what to look for, and what output you want. If the task involves a logged-in site, connect the relevant connector or provide the access path Juno should use.
