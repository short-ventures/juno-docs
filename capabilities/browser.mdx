Most AI tools can answer questions about the web, but they cannot reliably inspect the page in front of them.

Juno can use a browser when a task needs to look at websites, forms, pages, or browser-based tools directly.

This means you can ask it to research competitors, check a page, gather source material, or work through a web workflow without giving step-by-step clicks.

If the task needs a private site, Juno will use connected access when available or ask for what is missing.

## What it is for

- Visiting public websites and web apps
- Inspecting landing pages, forms and page content
- Gathering source material from URLs you provide
- Checking whether pages and assets load correctly
- Working in browser-based tools when access is available

## What to expect

Juno uses the browser when seeing the page is part of the work. Give it the URL, the decision you are trying to make, and the output you want.

For private sites, Juno may ask for a connector, credential, or access path before continuing.
