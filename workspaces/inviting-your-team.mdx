---
title: "Inviting your team"
description: "Add teammates so they can collaborate in a Juno workspace."
icon: "user-plus"
---

Invite teammates when they need to delegate work, review results, manage connectors, or help administer the workspace.

## Before inviting someone

- Confirm which workspace they should access
- Check whether they need connector or billing permissions
- Share any expectations for reviewing agent outputs

## Team responsibilities

Workspace owners and administrators are responsible for managing users, permissions, billing, connectors, and agent settings.

Only invite people who should be able to see the workspace's tasks, files, and connected context.
