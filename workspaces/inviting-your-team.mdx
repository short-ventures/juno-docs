---
title: "Inviting your team"
description: "Add teammates so they can collaborate in a Juno space."
icon: "user-plus"
---

Invite teammates when they need to delegate work, review results, manage plugins, or help administer the space.

## Before inviting someone

- Confirm which space they should access
- Check whether they need plugin or billing permissions
- Share any expectations for reviewing agent outputs

## Team responsibilities

Space owners and administrators are responsible for managing users, permissions, billing, plugins, and agent settings.

Only invite people who should be able to see the space's tasks, files, and connected context.
