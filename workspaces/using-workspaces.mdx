description: "Organize Juno work by space."

---

Spaces keep a team's tasks, connectors, files, memory, and billing context together.

## Use separate spaces for

- Different companies or brands
- Separate client accounts
- Teams with different tools or billing needs
- Projects that require different long-term context

## What belongs in a space

- Connected connectors
- Brand and company context
- Uploaded files and generated artifacts
- Scheduled work
- Team members who need access

Choose the space before delegating work so Juno uses the right context and credentials.
