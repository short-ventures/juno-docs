The last few years have shown us the super-human potential of AI agents, but the "jack of all trades, master of none" quip is clear.

For marketing teams, tools like Claude and ChatGPT lack the specialized capabilities to truly make growth work happen.

Where general AI tools might 10x a marketers output, we believe it's possible for Juno to get to 100x. We do this by making Juno a pro-active, independent coworker - not a chatbot.

Juno knows your brand inside out, connects to your tools and data and works 24/7 to help you grow it.

## When to use Juno

You should use Juno to add-value to any marketing work you may be doing. You'll naturally build the habit as you continue to build a relationship with Juno.

One of the best ways to begin seeing value is to consider recurring work that is labour intensive, for example:
- Building reports
- Creating landing pages
- Analyzing ad accounts
- Brand monitoring
- Lead generation

Juno can immediately help you with any of these tasks, scheduled repeating work and then simply reach out to you when it has results to show you.

## Is Juno secure to use?

With great power comes great responsibility, so we've designed Juno to be foundationally secure.

Any connectors you use with Juno are encrypted, secure and revokable at any point in time.

In addition, your agent's memory and conversations are isolated to your user profile and space.

Artifacts are shared through signed, encrypted links so that you can share outputs with your team securely.
