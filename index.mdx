---
title: "What is Juno?"
description: "Juno is an AI workspace for delegating real work across your apps, files, browser, and team."
---

Juno gives your team an agent that can take on operational work, use connected tools, and produce useful artifacts.

You delegate a task in plain language. Juno plans the work, asks for missing context when needed, uses the right capabilities, and keeps the output tied to your workspace.

## When to use Juno

Use Juno when the work has a clear outcome but requires steps across several systems. Common examples include research, reporting, campaign analysis, content updates, spreadsheet work, prospecting, and follow-up workflows.

For best results, describe the desired outcome, the source materials, the target format, and any constraints Juno should respect.
