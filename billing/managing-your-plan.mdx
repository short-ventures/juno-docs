Plans are managed from your team's settings. Team owners can review usage, start a trial or upgrade flow, open the billing portal, and manage auto top-up.

## What you can manage

- Current plan and billing state
- Usage remaining
- Daily and monthly credit balances
- Auto top-up
- Payment method and invoices through the billing portal
- Plan changes or cancellation

Only users with billing permissions can make plan changes.

## Auto top-up

Auto top-up helps your agent keep working when the team's credit balance reaches zero.

When auto top-up is enabled, Juno can add credits automatically instead of stopping the task at the balance limit. Use it when you want long-running or recurring work to continue without manual intervention.

Team owners can turn auto top-up on or off from **Settings** > **Billing**.

## Updating your card

Team owners can update the payment method from the billing portal.

1. Open Juno.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Open **Billing**.
4. Choose the billing portal action.
5. Update the payment method in Stripe.

If you do not see billing controls, ask the team owner to make the change.

## Changing your plan

Team owners can change plans from billing settings when plan changes are available for the workspace.

1. Open Juno.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Open **Billing**.
4. Review the available plan action.
5. Confirm the change in the checkout or billing portal flow.

Your usage allowance and billing state update after the billing provider confirms the change.

## Canceling your plan

You can cancel your plan at any time by downgrading to 'Free' in your billing settings.

1. Open Juno.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Open **Billing**.
4. Select **Change plan**
5. Switch to the free plan

The free plan will still enable you to communicate with your agent, with free daily credits.
