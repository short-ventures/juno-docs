  ## Artifact sharing

  You can now share generated artifacts with a read-only link. Open an artifact, click **Share**, then click **Copy** to send the link to teammates, clients, or other stakeholders who need to review the output.

  The share link gives viewers access to the artifact without letting them edit it. This makes it easier to hand off reports, files, and other Juno outputs while keeping the original artifact controlled in your workspace.
